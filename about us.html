Our Story

FOUNDED IN 2021 as a boutique home kitchen, FAIRYLAND CAKE Co. grew out of a passion for artisan baking and custom cakes, whimsical cupcakes, and detailed dessert spreads for birthdays and events.

Mission and Vision

To craft high quality, handcrafted baked goods that add charm to special celebrations while building a reliable local brand identity.

Target Audience

Local event hosts, parents planning birthdays, couples, and community members seeking artisan baked goods.

Reviews

High customer engagement and community satisfaction.
